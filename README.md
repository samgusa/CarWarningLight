## Description
**Find the answer to one of the world's most annoying questions: "What does that light on my dashboard mean?"**


Warning Light Camera is an innovative information and camera-based app that transforms how car owners 
identify and understand dashboard warning lights. With data on over 90 different dashboard warning lights, 
this app puts comprehensive automotive knowledge at your fingertips.
